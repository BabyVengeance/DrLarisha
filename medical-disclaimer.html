4. Physician-Administered Standard of Care

In accordance with HPCSA medical ethics and patient safety protocols, Dr Larisha Pather strictly adheres to non-delegated physician administration:

  • No Third-Party Injectors: You will always be evaluated and injected directly by Dr Larisha Pather. Injection procedures are never delegated to nurses, beauty therapists, or unqualified personnel.
  • Pharmacovigilance & Cold-Chain Integrity: All injectable neuromodulators, hyaluronic acid dermal fillers, and intravenous infusion constituents are sourced exclusively through licensed South African pharmaceutical distributors holding current SAHPRA approvals, stored under monitored clinical cold-chain conditions.
  • Informed Consent Process: Informed consent is an ongoing clinical dialogue. Prior to any needle entry or chemical application, you will be informed of treatment indications, alternative modalities, anticipated downtime, and realistic outcomes.

5. Procedural Risks & Individual Variability

Aesthetic medicine and physiological wellness therapies involve biological tissue interventions. Responses to injectables, chemical solutions, and metabolic medications vary based on individual genetic factors, vascular anatomy, skin Fitzpatrick phototype, and metabolic clearance rates:

  • Common, Transient Side Effects: Temporary erythema (redness), mild localized edema (swelling), sensitivity at needle puncture sites, and transient minor bruising are normal physiological responses that typically resolve within 2 to 7 days.
  • Rare & Serious Complications: As with all medical injectable procedures, rare adverse events can include localized infection, granuloma formation, asymmetric muscular response, or vascular occlusion. Dr Larisha maintains dedicated emergency reversal agents (including pharmaceutical-grade hyaluronidase enzyme) on-site in theater for immediate clinical management if required.
  • Lifestyle Factors: High UV sun exposure, nicotine consumption, alcohol intake, strenuous exercise within 24 hours of treatment, and non-compliance with post-care instructions directly impair aesthetic longevity and tissue recovery.

6. Absolute & Relative Medical Contraindications

Certain clinical treatments may be contraindicated or deferred for patient safety. Patients have an absolute ethical and clinical duty to fully disclose all current medications, medical diagnoses, and health conditions, including but not limited to:

  • Pregnancy & Lactation: Aesthetic injectables (BTOX and Dermal Fillers) and systemic metabolic weight-loss medications are strictly contraindicated during pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Neuromuscular Disorders: Conditions such as Myasthenia Gravis, Lambert-Eaton syndrome, or ALS preclude the administration of botulinum toxin products.
  • Cutaneous Infections: Active herpes simplex (cold sores), active acne breakouts, or bacterial infections at or near the proposed injection sites require treatment deferral until fully resolved.
  • Anticoagulation & Bleeding Disorders: Use of blood-thinning pharmaceuticals (Warfarin, Xarelto, Aspirin) or high-dose herbal supplements (Omega-3, Ginkgo Biloba) increases bruising risk and must be reviewed prior to treatment.

In adherence to the HPCSA Guidelines on Advertising and Ethical Rules (Booklet 2 & 9):
