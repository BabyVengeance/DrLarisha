Patient Care & Guidance

Preparing for Your Treatment at Parklands Hospital

To ensure the smoothest outcome and minimal bruising, Dr Larisha recommends following these standard clinical precautions prior to your injectable or peel appointment:

Before Injectable Procedures (Lip Fillers & BTOX)

Avoid blood-thinning supplements (Vitamin E, high-dose Omega-3, Ginkgo Biloba) and anti-inflammatories for 48 hours prior. Refrain from alcohol for 24 hours. Ensure you arrive with clean skin free of heavy makeup.

Before IV Wellness & Micronutrient Infusions

Drink plenty of water before your appointment to ensure optimal venous access. Eat a light meal 1 to 2 hours before arriving to maintain stable blood glucose levels throughout your infusion.
