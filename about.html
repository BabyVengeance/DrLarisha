Medical Foundations

Physician-Led Aesthetics Grounded in Clinical Science

Dr Larisha Pather is a registered medical practitioner whose practice bridges advanced aesthetic medicine, dermal rejuvenation, and metabolic wellness. Operating from private consulting rooms in Netcare Parklands Hospital, she offers patients an atmosphere of calm discretion coupled with rigorous hospital-grade clinical standards.

Unlike standard commercial beauty salons or high-volume aesthetic chains where non-medical operators perform invasive injectables, every consultation, facial assessment, and procedure at Dr Pather's clinic is personally conducted by Dr Larisha herself. This ensures complete anatomical accuracy, strict infection control, and tailored patient outcomes.

“I believe in neutral, balanced beauty. Not loud. Simple but captures the eye. Aesthetic medicine should honour your natural facial architecture, not distort it. My goal is for you to look rested, revitalised, and unmistakably yourself.”
— Dr Larisha Pather, MBChB

Her holistic clinical focus recognises that radiant skin and facial balance cannot be separated from internal wellness. By integrating high-dose IV antioxidant infusions, physician-guided metabolic weight programs, and medical-grade skincare alongside targeted neuromodulators and dermal fillers, Dr Pather delivers sustainable, long-term health.
